# hack-c392aa23-devspark
Hackathon team repository for DevSpark

Current track: Management

Career Quest is an AI‑driven employee development navigator. 
We solve the problem of fragmented employee development: people receive HR activities without a clear connection to their career goal and the subsequent requirements, so they often complete training formally or skip voluntary opportunities.
Career Quest links the employee’s profile, participation history, and requirements for the next grade to suggest suitable development steps with an explanation: what skill needs to be developed, why this activity is suitable, and how it will bring them closer to the goal.
